# Demo Questions

Dung cac cau hoi nay sau khi index tai lieu trong `data/sample_documents/`:

1. Nhan vien co bao nhieu ngay nghi phep nam?
2. Muon nghi phep thi can dang ky truoc bao lau?
3. Chinh sach lam viec tu xa nhu the nao?
4. Nhan vien co duoc chia se tai lieu noi bo ra ngoai khong?
5. Phong Tai chinh xu ly de nghi tam ung trong bao lau?
6. Ho so hoan ung can nhung gi?
7. Thanh toan nha cung cap tren 50 trieu can ai phe duyet?
8. Chung tu tai chinh duoc luu tru toi thieu bao lau?
9. Ai phu trach cau hoi ve nghi phep nam?
10. Cong ty co chinh sach mua bao hiem nha o khong?

Expected behavior:
- Cau 1-9 phai tra loi co can cu va hien nguon.
- Cau 10 khong co trong tai lieu, he thong nen noi chua tim thay du thong tin.
